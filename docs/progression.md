# Campaign progression

Classic campaigns reward mastery of different maps and difficulties. A run earns
one research point per new threshold of 5, 10, 15 or 20 waves cleared. Progress
counts on easier difficulties as well. Clearing wave 10 on Veteran for the
first time earns up to six points on that map. A defeat records every cleared
wave. Repeating a personal best pays no additional points.

The four maps offer 48 points in total. The Workshop has four tracks with five
ranks each. Ranks cost 1, 2, 2, 3 and 4 research points, so a player can buy all
20 ranks after mastering every map on Veteran. The first points offer a choice
between immediate buying power and bonuses that benefit later waves.

| Track | Per rank | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Firepower | +3% tower damage | +15% |
| Reload | −2% of base reload time | −10% |
| Targeting range | +3 units of base tower range | +15 units |
| Starting gold | +10 gold | +50 gold |

Research modifies tower base stats before in-mission upgrades, and can be
refunded freely between runs. Active missions freeze the selected research in
their checkpoint, so a purchase or refund becomes effective on the next start
or retry. Quick and Remix do not award mastery or use research. Both modes
retain their existing XP and tower modules.

The local profile stores the best cleared wave for each map and difficulty and
the ranks purchased. The unspent balance is calculated from those values.
Older profiles retain XP and unlocks, and begin recording mastery with their
next finished Classic campaign. Older checkpoints retain their original setup.
